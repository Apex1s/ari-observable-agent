# EMINES admissions, fees and scholarships

## Admission routes
EMINES admits students by competitive entrance exam (concours). There are two main entry points: the integrated preparatory cycle after a scientific baccalaureate, and the engineering cycle after preparatory classes or a bachelor's degree. Results are published separately for Moroccan baccalaureates, foreign baccalaureates, CPGE Morocco and CPGE sub-Saharan Africa. Applications are submitted online on the EMINES website.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/fr/ https://emines-ingenieur.org/admission/admission-post-bac

## Entrance exam for the integrated preparatory cycle (after the baccalaureate)
The preparatory cycle is open to holders of a scientific baccalaureate. The best academic files are selected to sit the exam. The admissibility stage is a multiple-choice written test in mathematical tools (QCM "Mathématiques outils"). The admission stage consists of oral exams in mathematics, physical sciences and scientific reasoning.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/admission/admission-post-bac

## Preparatory cycle calendar (2026 session)
Online applications ran from January to Monday 1 June 2026. Moroccan baccalaureate transcripts were due by Monday 22 June 2026; foreign transcripts as soon as available. Exams were held in Ben Guerir in early July 2026, with results in mid-July 2026. Dates change each year, so check the admissions page for the next session.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/admission/admission-post-bac

## Entrance exam and requirements for the engineering cycle
Bac+2 applicants need two years of scientific preparatory classes (CPGE) in MP, PSI or TSI. Bac+3 applicants need a fundamental licence in mathematics, mathematics and computer science, applied mathematics or physical sciences, or a foreign equivalent. Selection has three steps: file review, a written QCM in mathematical tools, then oral exams in mathematics, physical sciences and scientific reasoning. For 2026, applications ran from January to 11 May, exams were held in Ben Guerir from late May to early June, and results came in mid-June.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/admission/admission-cycle-inge

## Number of places
The school's current admissions pages do not state the number of places. An older English admissions page gave 30 to 50 places for the preparatory cycle and 50 to 70 places for the engineering cycle; treat these as dated figures.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/admissions

## Tuition fees
The French fees page lists a one-time first registration fee of 5,000 dirhams and annual tuition of 78,750 dirhams for the preparatory and engineering cycles. A single room in a four-bedroom campus apartment costs 1,500 dirhams per month. The page gives no year, and an older English page shows lower figures (up to 75,000 dirhams tuition and 1,000 dirhams rent), so confirm current fees with the school.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/frais-de-scolarite https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/education/tuition-fees-and-scholarships

## Scholarships
Excellence scholarships cover 25, 50, 75 or 100 percent of tuition and are awarded by rank in the entrance exam. Social scholarships can cover up to 100 percent of total costs, including tuition, registration, housing and food, after review by the Scholarship Commission. The two types can be combined. Scholarship decisions are announced with the admission results.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/frais-de-scolarite https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/en/admissions/admission-to-integrated-preparatory-cycle-post-bac
