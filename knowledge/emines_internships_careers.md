# EMINES internships, international experience and careers

## Internship requirements
Every EMINES engineering student must complete at least 42 weeks of company immersion and spend at least 3 months abroad. An optional gap year (année de césure) with a long company internship is possible between the second and third years of the engineering cycle.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/formation/formation-ingenieur/les-stages-dans-la-formation

## Internships by year
In year 1 of the engineering cycle, students do a 4-week worker internship (stage ouvrier), one week of industrial visits within the Industrial Management Initiation module (IMI) and one week of geology fieldwork. In year 2 they do a one-week shadowing internship with a manager and a 12-week research initiation internship abroad. In year 3 they do 1 to 3 weeks of option industrial visits and a 24-week option project (travail d'option) in a company.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/formation/formation-ingenieur/les-stages-dans-la-formation

## International experience
An international experience is mandatory for all students. The second-year internship takes place in an emerging country; the school lists Brazil, Chile, India, China and Malaysia. Students may also take option trips with industrial visits or exchanges, depending on their option.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/formation/formation-ingenieur/l-international-et-les-langues

## Industry links
Final-year option studies are carried out with host organisations on real problems. In the class of 2021, host organisations included OCP Group and OCP Solutions, Managem, BCP Group, BCG and BCG Gamma, Procter & Gamble, Unilever, L'Oréal Maroc, Bayer, LafargeHolcim, Sonasid, Stellantis, Orano, JESA, CDG Capital and AgriEdge. OCP funds the OCP Industrial Optimization Chair at the school.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/images/Documents/brochure_2021.pdf https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/entreprises/devenir-partenaire/chaires-industrielles

## Graduate outcomes
EMINES reports that 84 percent of graduates from the classes of 2017 to 2025 found a job within 3 months of graduation. It reports an average net monthly salary of 17,100 dirhams at graduation for the class finishing in September 2025. It also reports averages of 30,200 dirhams after 4 years (classes 2017 to 2021) and 45,600 dirhams after 7 years (classes 2017 and 2018).
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/chiffres-cles

## Alumni
EMINES counts 432 engineering alumni from the classes of 2017 to 2025 (figure as of January 2026). Of these, 110 work abroad: 81 in Europe, 13 in Asia, 10 in North America and 6 in Africa. The school reports 12 active startups created.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/chiffres-cles

## Target sectors
The EMINES careers page says graduates are trained as industrial managers for companies in Morocco. It names sectors such as supply chain (automotive, aeronautics, agribusiness, retail, logistics, consulting) and mining and construction (citing OCP, Managem and Lafarge). This page is older and lists some fields that are not current options.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/corporate/opportunities-and-careers
