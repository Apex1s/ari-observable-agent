# UM6P Schools, Colleges and Programmes

## How programmes are organised
UM6P groups its schools into four clusters: Science and Technology; Medical and Paramedical; Business and Management; and Humanities, Economics and Social Sciences. The names and list of schools change over time, so check the official academic offer for the current list.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/academic-offer

## Science and Technology cluster
This cluster includes the School of Industrial Management (EMINES), the College of Computing, the Green Tech Institute, and the Institute of Science, Technology and Innovation (IST&I). It also includes the School of Agriculture, Fertilization and Environmental Sciences (ESAFE) and the School of Architecture, Planning and Design (SAP+D). The 1337 coding school, Technix, the Institute of Applied Digital Technologies and the Institute of Education Sciences are also listed in this cluster.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/academic-offer

## Humanities, Economics and Social Sciences cluster
This cluster includes the Faculty of Governance, Economics and Social Sciences (FGSES), the Public Policy School, and the Africa Institute for Research in Economics and Social Sciences. Undergraduate options listed include Applied Economics, Political Science and International Relations.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/academic-offer, https://um6p.ma/en/education/overview

## Business and Management cluster
This cluster includes the Africa Business School (ABS), the School of Collective Intelligence (SCI), and the School of Hospitality Business and Management (SHBM). It also lists the Mahir Center, the Moroccan Center for Game Theory and the Story School.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/academic-offer

## Medical and Paramedical cluster
This cluster includes the Faculty of Medical Sciences (FMS) and the Institute of Biological Sciences. Listed programmes include a Doctorate in Medicine and a Doctorate in Pharmacy, both taught in English, and a Bachelor in Nursing Sciences. The medical faculty is linked to UM6P's Smart Health Care City.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/academic-offer, https://shcc.um6p.ma/um6p-hospitals/

## EMINES – School of Industrial Management
EMINES (School of Industrial Management) is a UM6P school on the Ben Guerir campus. It was the first UM6P school, opening in 2013. Detailed information about EMINES is in the EMINES files of this knowledge base.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Mohammed_VI_Polytechnic, https://www.um6p.ma/en/academic-offer

## Admissions
UM6P publishes its programmes and application calendar on its admissions page. The 2026-2027 intake listed post-baccalaureate, master's and engineering programmes, with closing dates in spring 2026. Ari cannot confirm whether a programme is still open; visitors should check the admissions page.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/admissions, https://um6p.ma/en/enrollment/admissions
