# EMINES overview

## What EMINES is
EMINES – School of Industrial Management is a school of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), on the UM6P campus in Ben Guerir, Morocco. It trains engineers in industrial management, also called engineer-managers (ingénieurs managers). The school's website describes EMINES as "the first stone of UM6P". EMINES describes itself as resolutely oriented towards the business world.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/en https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/l-emines

## History
EMINES has offered undergraduate and graduate programmes since September 2013. The UM6P section of the EMINES website lists EMINES with the year 2013. The EMINES project team was formed from June 2010, according to the school's leadership page. The first engineering graduates counted in the school's alumni figures are the class of 2017.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/offre-de-formation https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/emines/mohammed-vi-polytechnic-university https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/board https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/chiffres-cles

## Mission
EMINES aims to train future business leaders and engineer-managers with solid scientific and technical foundations, prepared for entrepreneurship in Morocco and across Africa. It also aims to develop research that answers concrete needs of African companies. Its approach relies on project-based teaching, at least 42 weeks of company internships (about one third abroad), industry partnerships and an entrepreneurial culture.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/l-emines

## Relation to UM6P and OCP
EMINES is part of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (Université Mohammed VI Polytechnique, UM6P). The EMINES website describes UM6P as a private, non-profit university with a pan-African and international vocation, incubated by OCP S.A. OCP also funds an industrial chair at EMINES, the OCP Industrial Optimization Chair (Chaire OCP d'Optimisation industrielle).
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/umvip https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/entreprises/devenir-partenaire/chaires-industrielles

## Academic partners
EMINES has a privileged partnership with MINES Paris - PSL (formerly MINES ParisTech, École des Mines de Paris): shared teaching materials, joint research and co-supervision of options. A five-year chair with MINES Paris - PSL was signed in 2024. École polytechnique (France) supports the Data Science option through a Data Science & Industrial Processes chair signed in 2018 and renewed in 2022 for five years. Université Paris II Panthéon-Assas, Université Paris Dauphine and Université Clermont Auvergne are partners for doctoral co-supervision.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/international/partenariats-internationaux https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/l-emines

## Accreditation and recognition
The school was authorised on 28/11/2014 (authorisation n° 404/13). The Industrial Management programme (preparatory and engineering cycles) was authorised on 03/06/2015 and accredited on 19/10/2023 (n° 04/2066). The EMINES diploma received state recognition, with equivalence to state diplomas, on 10/05/2023 (n° 2.23.424).
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/l-emines/accreditations

## Key figures (2025-2026)
EMINES reports 575 students in 2025-2026. Teaching staff include 17 permanent full-time faculty-researchers, 10 part-time affiliated professors and about 80 lecturers and visiting instructors. The school counts 432 engineering alumni (classes 2017 to 2025), of whom 110 work abroad. It reports 12 active startups created by its community.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/chiffres-cles
