# EMINES overview

## The school
EMINES – School of Industrial Management is a school of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P), located on the university's campus in Ben Guerir, Morocco. It trains engineers in industrial management: operations, supply chain, data science and decision-making for industry.

## Engineering cycle
The engineering cycle lasts three years. Students follow a common core in industrial engineering, management and applied mathematics, then choose a track in the final year, for example Data Science. Each year includes an internship, and the final year ends with an end-of-studies project carried out in a company. (Sample description: check the official programme brochure.)

## Student life
Students live on the UM6P campus. Clubs cover robotics, entrepreneurship, sports and culture. The robotics club built Ari as a cross-functional team project. (Sample description.)

## Contacting the school
For admissions questions, visitors should go to the reception desk in the main hall or use the contact page on the official EMINES website. Ari can guide you to the reception desk.
