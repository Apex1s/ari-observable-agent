# Student life at EMINES

## Campus
EMINES is located on the 35-hectare campus of Mohammed VI Polytechnic University in Ben Guerir, near Marrakech. The school's website presents Ben Guerir as a future green city (ville verte). The campus was designed by the architects Ricardo Bofill and Elie Mouyal.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/campus/campus-umvip https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/life-at-emines

## Student associations and clubs
Student extracurricular life is run by three bureaus: the Students' Bureau (Bureau des Élèves, BDE), the Arts Bureau (Bureau des Arts, BDA) and the Sports Bureau (Bureau des Sports, BDS). Students have also created clubs, such as the E-Tech Club and an astrology club, according to the school's campus page.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/campus/campus-umvip

## Sports facilities
The campus has about 5,000 square metres of outdoor sports grounds, including 2 grass mini-football pitches and 4 multi-sport courts for tennis, basketball, volleyball and handball. There is a 25-metre semi-Olympic pool and a learning pool. Indoor facilities include a fitness room, a weight room, a dance room and a combat sports room.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/campus/campus-umvip

## Housing
EMINES students live in on-campus apartments of four private rooms sharing a living room and kitchenette. Each furnished room has its own bathroom, and units have high-speed internet. Residences have a TV lounge, a games area (billiards, table football, table tennis) and a laundry, and some rooms are adapted for people with reduced mobility. The French fees page lists rent of 1,500 dirhams per month for a single room.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/life-at-emines/accomodation https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/frais-de-scolarite

## Meals
A campus restaurant serves breakfast, lunch and dinner, and campus cafeterias also serve meals. Apartments have equipped kitchens, so students can also cook for themselves.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/life-at-emines/accomodation https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/frais-de-scolarite

## Library
The EMINES library holds about 12,000 books covering the school's fields, plus a general collection of novels, literature, magazines and history. Its catalogue can be searched from anywhere on campus, and librarians help with research. The website does not list opening hours.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/campus/bibliotheque

## Events
EMINES students take part in the Cartel des Mines, a gathering of European and Moroccan mining engineering schools; press reported that EMINES first took part in 2014 in Saint-Étienne and again in 2015 in Nantes. The school's news page also lists Cartel des Mines 2024 results (Nantes). Each year, final-year students publicly defend their option studies, and the school invites partners to attend.
