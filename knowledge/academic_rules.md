# Academic rules (sample data)

## Attendance
Attendance is mandatory for all classes, tutorials and lab sessions. A student who misses more than 20% of the sessions of a course without a justified absence cannot sit the final exam of that course. Absences are justified by submitting a medical certificate or official document to the academic office within 48 hours.

## Exams and resits
Each course is graded out of 20. A course is validated with a final grade of 10/20 or more. Students who fail a course may take one resit exam, held in July; the resit grade replaces the exam grade, capped at 12/20.

## Grading
The final grade of a course combines continuous assessment (40%) and the final exam (60%), unless the course syllabus states otherwise. Grades are published on the student portal within three weeks after the exam.

## Internships
Students complete one internship per year. The first-year internship lasts at least 4 weeks, the second-year internship at least 8 weeks, and the end-of-studies project lasts 6 months. Internship agreements must be signed by the company, the student and the internship office before the internship starts.

## Academic office
The academic office is in room A-104, on the first floor of building A. It is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 16:00 and handles certificates, absence justifications and transcripts.
