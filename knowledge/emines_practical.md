# EMINES practical information

## Address
The official address is EMINES – Université Mohammed VI Polytechnique, Lot 660, Hay Moulay Rachid, 43150 Ben Guerir, Morocco. The school is on the UM6P campus in Ben Guerir.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/contact https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/plan-d-acces

## Getting there
By car, Ben Guerir is about 40 minutes north of Marrakech on the Casablanca motorway. By train, Ben Guerir station is about 50 minutes from Marrakech and about 2 hours 10 minutes from Casablanca, with 8 trains per day. Marrakech airport is about 1 hour away and Casablanca airport about 2 hours away.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/a-propos/plan-d-acces

## Opening hours
The EMINES contact page gives opening hours of Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 19:00.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/contact

## Contacting the school
The official website is www.emines-ingenieur.org, with a contact form on its contact page and an online application page for admissions. EMINES is on Facebook (EMINES.UM6P), X/Twitter (EMINESUM6P) and LinkedIn (emines-um6p). The website does not display a public e-mail address or phone number in plain text.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/contact https://emines-ingenieur.org/admission/candidature-online
