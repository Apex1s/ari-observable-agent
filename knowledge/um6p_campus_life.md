# UM6P Ben Guerir Campus Life

## Learning Center (library)
The UM6P Learning Center is the university library, with both physical and digital resources. UM6P says it holds over 13,000 resources and recently added new books and a new reading space. Its spaces include a reading room, an exhibition space and a mezzanine. Staff offer reference, consultation and research help. No official opening hours were found; visitors should ask the Learning Center desk.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/learning-center, https://visit.um6p.ma/bibliotheque_eng.php, https://rd20.um6p.ma/lodging

## Student housing
On-campus student housing is made of apartments with four single bedrooms. Each bedroom has a private bathroom, and residents share a living area and a kitchenette. Shared amenities include a TV lounge, a game room and a laundry service.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/benguerir-morocco, https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/overview

## Dining
The main university restaurant serves breakfast, lunch and dinner. UM6P also lists several other restaurants on campus: Restaurant T1, Restaurant CCI, Restaurant Friends, Restaurant Rihla at the School of Hospitality Business and Management (SHBM), and Zen Eat, which serves Asian cuisine. There is also a food truck area, and cafés and shops on campus. No official opening hours were found.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/overview, https://ias.um6p.ma/campus/

## Sports complex
The sports complex has about 5,000 m² of outdoor fields, including two turf mini-football pitches and courts for tennis, basketball, handball and volleyball. Indoors there is a semi-Olympic 25 m swimming pool, a learning pool, cardio and weight rooms, a dance hall and a combat (martial arts) room. Facilities are booked through the Campus Plus (Campus+) app. The university sports department also runs teams that compete at national level.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/overview, https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/life-at-emines/um6p-campus, https://visit.um6p.ma/complexe_sportif_eng.php

## Health services
The campus has a medical center (health center). UM6P says it offers consultations, basic treatment and preventive care for the university community. No official opening hours were found.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/overview, https://ias.um6p.ma/campus/

## Other services
The campus has banking and postal services, parks and green spaces, and an open-air social space at its centre called The Pergola. The Career Center offers coaching workshops, one-to-one guidance and networking events.
Source: https://ias.um6p.ma/campus/, https://um6p.ma/en/career-center

## Clubs and student activities
Student clubs are supported by the Student Organizations, Leadership and Engagement office (SOLE). Each year SOLE runs X-ibition, a fair where clubs present their activities; its 5th edition took place on the Ben Guerir campus. Clubs also organise events such as a charity gala during Ramadan. Schools have their own student bodies; for example, EMINES has a Students Bureau (BDE), an Arts Bureau (BDA) and a Sports Bureau (BDS).
Source: https://um6p.ma/5th-edition-x-ibition-showcased-student-clubs-and-activities-across-um6p-campuses, https://um6p.ma/charity-gala-night-mobilized-community-members-ramadan-solidarity-initiative, https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/life-at-emines/um6p-campus

## Events and the Campus+ app
UM6P hosts public events on the Ben Guerir campus, such as the Deep Tech Summit and Science Week in 2025. The Campus+ app lets people receive campus news, join public events and book sports facilities.
Source: https://um6p.ma/fr/espace-informatif/evenements, https://um6p.ma/en/benguerir-morocco, https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/overview
