# UM6P Practical Information: Getting There and Contact

## Address
The Ben Guerir campus address is: Université Mohammed VI Polytechnique, Lot 660, Hay Moulay Rachid, Ben Guerir 43150, Morocco.
Source: https://physicsschool.um6p.ma/, https://ias.um6p.ma/campus/

## From Marrakech
Ben Guerir is about 60 km north of Marrakech. UM6P says the drive takes about 40 minutes on the Casablanca highway. By train, Marrakech to Ben Guerir takes about 45 to 50 minutes, with roughly one train per hour. Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) is about 95 km away, roughly 1h15 by car.
Source: https://ias.um6p.ma/campus/, https://um6p.ma/en/benguerir-morocco, https://rd20.um6p.ma/lodging

## From Casablanca
By train, Casablanca to Ben Guerir takes about 1h56, with roughly one train every two hours. Casablanca Mohammed V Airport (CMN) is about 200 km away, roughly 2h15 by car. The airport has its own train station, so travellers can take the train directly towards Ben Guerir.
Source: https://rd20.um6p.ma/lodging, https://rd20.um6p.ma/faqs

## Train and taxi
UM6P event pages recommend the train as the best way to reach Ben Guerir. From the train station in Ben Guerir, visitors then take a taxi to the campus.
Source: https://rd20.um6p.ma/lodging, https://rd20.um6p.ma/faqs

## Hotel near campus
For visitors, UM6P event pages mention on-campus rooms and the DoubleThree by Hilton hotel as accommodation options.
Source: https://rd20.um6p.ma/lodging

## Official website and contact
The official website is https://um6p.ma. The general contact e-mail is contact@um6p.ma and the main switchboard is +212 525-073 100. Many schools and units have their own contact pages on their um6p.ma sub-sites.
Source: https://visit.um6p.ma/learn/um6p_map.php, https://um6p.ma/en/vision

## Admissions information
Programmes and application dates are published on the UM6P admissions page. Questions about a specific school are best sent through that school's own contact page.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/admissions
