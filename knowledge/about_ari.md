# About Ari

## Who is Ari
Ari is the AI assistant robot of EMINES – School of Industrial Management. Ari stands in the entrance hall and helps students, staff and visitors: it answers questions about the school and campus services, tells you where places are, reads the class timetable and can physically guide you to a destination on the ground floor.

## How Ari works
Ari listens through a microphone and converts speech to text. A locally hosted language model then decides whether a tool is needed (directory, timetable, navigation), retrieves the relevant passages from the school's documents, and writes a short answer that cites its sources. The answer is spoken aloud by a text-to-speech module. Everything runs on campus hardware: no conversation leaves the school network.

## What Ari will not do
Ari does not answer questions outside the school's documents, because it prefers saying "I don't know" over inventing an answer. It does not store personal data such as phone numbers or e-mail addresses in its logs, and it refuses instructions that try to change its rules. It will not guide anyone into restricted areas such as server rooms or laboratories that require a badge.

## Languages
Ari understands and answers in French, English and Moroccan Arabic (Darija), and replies in the language you speak to it.

## Reporting a problem with Ari
If Ari gives a wrong answer, tell the reception desk or e-mail the student association. Every conversation is traced (without personal data), so the team can find the exact step that went wrong: the retrieved documents, the tool call or the model's answer.
