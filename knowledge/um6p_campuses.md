# UM6P Campuses

## Where the UM6P campuses are (summary)
UM6P has three campuses in Morocco: Ben Guerir (the main campus, where EMINES is), Rabat (in Salé) and Laayoune in the south. Separately, it lists international hubs and branches abroad, which are not campuses in Morocco.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/campuses https://um6p.ma/en/um6p-global-hubs

## Ben Guerir campus (main campus)
The Ben Guerir campus is the main hub of UM6P. It sits in the heart of the Mohammed VI Green City (Ville Verte Mohammed VI), near Marrakech. UM6P gives the campus area as 80 hectares. It was designed by the architects Ricardo Bofill and Elie Mouyal. The postal address is Lot 660, Hay Moulay Rachid, Ben Guerir 43150, Morocco.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/campuses, https://um6p.ma/en/benguerir-morocco, https://ias.um6p.ma/campus/

## Mohammed VI Green City
The Mohammed VI Green City in Ben Guerir was launched in 2009 by King Mohammed VI. UM6P describes it as part of OCP Group's large urban development projects, designed to become a university hub. It is presented as a model of sustainable urban planning.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/benguerir-morocco, https://ias.um6p.ma/campus/

## Rabat campus
The Rabat campus is in the municipality of Salé, in the university district of the capital. UM6P says it was established in 2020 and covers four hectares. The university says this campus connects it with national institutions, policy think tanks and diplomatic bodies. The Africa Business School and the Faculty of Governance, Economics and Social Sciences (FGSES) have a presence there.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/campuses, https://abs.um6p.ma/our-campus/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Mohammed_VI_Polytechnic

## Laayoune campus
UM6P also has a campus in Laayoune (Laâyoune), in southern Morocco. The university describes it as a regional antenna of UM6P in the south of the country.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/facilities/campuses, https://um6p.ma/en/node/115

## Global hubs abroad
Outside Morocco, UM6P lists hubs and branches in France (Paris), Canada (Montreal), the United States (New York) and Côte d'Ivoire. These are not campuses in Morocco.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/um6p-global-hubs
