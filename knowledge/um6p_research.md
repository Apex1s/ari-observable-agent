# UM6P Research and Innovation

## Research priorities
UM6P says its research is anchored in renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, artificial intelligence and health. In 2025, the press reported over 6,600 indexed publications by UM6P between 2020 and 2025.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/um6p/about-the-university, https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/10/262858/um6p-rises-to-top-400-global-universities-in-the-2026-rankings/

## Toubkal supercomputer
Toubkal is a supercomputer hosted at UM6P in Ben Guerir, named after Morocco's highest Atlas peak. It is run by the African Supercomputing Center, which opened in 2021. The Toubkal website cites a 98th place on the TOP500 list of supercomputers. It also says a 1 MWp solar installation can power it with clean electricity at peak production.
Source: https://toubkal.um6p.ma/, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Mohammed_VI_Polytechnic

## Green Energy Park
The Green Energy Park in Ben Guerir is a solar energy testing and training platform. It was inaugurated by King Mohammed VI in January 2017, together with the university. Its Green and Smart Building Park platform works on green buildings, energy efficiency, smart grids and sustainable mobility. It is supported by UM6P, the Research Institute for Solar Energy and New Energies (IRESEN) and KOICA.
Source: https://boursenews.ma/article/actualite/revue-de-presse-du-13-janvier-2017, https://www.um6p.ma/en/gep-inaugurated-green-smart-building-park-platform

## Green Tech Institute
The Green Tech Institute (GTI) is a UM6P institute working on renewable energy, smart grids and green manufacturing.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/green-tech-institute

## StartGate (startup campus)
StartGate is UM6P's startup and innovation campus in Ben Guerir, created in 2020. It hosts several incubation and acceleration programmes and is open to startups from outside UM6P. In 2023, UM6P reported that more than 500 startup projects had been supported. Programmes include U-Founders (incubation), The Forge (launched in December 2024, starting March 2025) and NextAfrica, which alternates between StartGate and STATION F in Paris.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/more-500-start-ups-accompanied-um6p, https://um6p.ma/en/um6p-unveiled-forge-new-entrepreneurial-program-shape-future-moroccan-startups, https://launchbaseafrica.com/2025/02/28/um6p-startup-programme-plants-flag-at-worlds-largest-tech-hub-were-alternating-between-station-f-and-startgate/

## Industry partnerships
UM6P runs joint centres with industry. With Oracle and the Moroccan government, it set up an Oracle Lab in Casablanca in 2022. In November 2024, the Africa Center of Manufacturing Excellence (ACME) was created in Nouaceur with Boeing and the Moroccan government. UM6P and OCP Group also set up a Sulfur Excellence Center.
Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Mohammed_VI_Polytechnic, https://um6p.ma/en/um6p/about-the-university

## Policy research
The Policy Center for the New South, a Moroccan think tank, is housed at UM6P's Rabat campus.
