# Campus services (sample data)

## Library
The library is on the first floor of building A. It is open Monday to Friday from 8:30 to 22:00 and Saturday from 9:00 to 18:00. Students can borrow up to 5 books for 14 days with their student card. Group study rooms are booked at the library desk, for a maximum of 2 hours per booking.

## FabLab
The FabLab (fabrication laboratory) is on the ground floor of building B. It has 3D printers, a laser cutter and electronics benches. Access requires completing the FabLab safety training, held every Wednesday at 14:00. Opening hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00.

## Cafeteria
The cafeteria is on the ground floor of building C. Breakfast is served from 7:30 to 9:30, lunch from 12:00 to 14:30 and dinner from 19:00 to 21:00. Payment is made with the student card.

## IT help desk
The IT help desk is in room A-012, on the ground floor of building A. It handles Wi-Fi access, student e-mail accounts and password resets. Opening hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 17:00. For a password reset, bring your student card.

## Reception desk
The reception desk is in the main hall, next to Ari. It is open every day from 8:00 to 20:00 and handles visitor badges, lost and found, and general information.

## Infirmary
The infirmary is on the ground floor of building C, next to the cafeteria. A nurse is present Monday to Friday from 8:00 to 18:00. In an emergency outside these hours, call campus security from any red phone in the corridors.
