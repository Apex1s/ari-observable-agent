# UM6P – Mohammed VI Polytechnic University: Overview

## What UM6P is
Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (Université Mohammed VI Polytechnique, UM6P) is a private, non-profit research university in Morocco. UM6P describes itself as an institution oriented towards applied research and innovation, with a focus on Africa. Its main campus is in Ben Guerir, inside the Mohammed VI Green City (Ville Verte Mohammed VI).
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/vision, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Mohammed_VI_Polytechnic

## Founding and history
The university grew out of the Mohammed VI Green City project in Ben Guerir, launched in 2009 by King Mohammed VI. Its first phase was operating from October 2013, when it welcomed its first students. King Mohammed VI officially inaugurated the university on 12 January 2017. On the same visit the King also inaugurated the Green Energy Park, a solar energy testing and training platform.

## Link with OCP
UM6P is backed by the OCP Foundation (Fondation OCP), the foundation of the OCP Group, the Moroccan phosphate company. The Green City of Ben Guerir is described by UM6P as one of OCP Group's large urban development projects. In 2023, the press reported that the OCP Foundation had put about MAD 10 billion (around 986 million US dollars) into the university. UM6P and the OCP Foundation also run joint programmes, such as a partnership with UNESCO for 2025-2027.

## Mission
UM6P says its core mission is to develop knowledge, science and technology. It also lists training future leaders, promoting research, sharing values of social responsibility and sustainable development, and building lasting partnerships. The university says its research is anchored in renewable energy, sustainable agriculture, artificial intelligence and health.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/vision, https://um6p.ma/en/um6p/about-the-university

## Leadership
According to the university's governance page, Hicham El Habti is the President of UM6P. Abdelhadi Sohib is listed as Director General.
Source: https://www.um6p.ma/en/um6p-governance

## Key numbers
Figures differ between sources and years, so always give the year. In 2025, UM6P's global hubs page said the university enrolls over 7,200 students in 42 programmes, including nearly 1,000 doctoral candidates, with 61% women. In October 2025, Morocco World News reported 8,756 students from 40 nationalities, 379 permanent professors and about 1,300 doctoral students. The same 2025 report said UM6P produced over 6,600 indexed scientific publications between 2020 and 2025.
Source: https://um6p.ma/en/um6p-global-hubs, https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/10/262858/um6p-rises-to-top-400-global-universities-in-the-2026-rankings/

## Rankings
In October 2025, the press reported that UM6P entered the top 400 universities worldwide in the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2026.
Source: https://www.moroccoworldnews.com/2025/10/262858/um6p-rises-to-top-400-global-universities-in-the-2026-rankings/, https://ecoactu.ma/um6p-entre-dans-le-top-400-des-universites-mondiales/

## International presence
Besides its Moroccan campuses, UM6P has international branches and hubs. UM6P lists branches in France, Canada, the United States and Côte d'Ivoire. The France branch was UM6P's first international branch.
