# EMINES programmes

## Programme overview
EMINES currently lists four offers: the integrated preparatory cycle, the engineering cycle, the industrial PhD and executive education. The engineering programme in industrial management (management industriel) takes 3 to 5 years depending on the entry point. Teaching combines lectures, team projects, field immersion and learning by doing.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/offre-de-formation https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/education/engineering-program

## Integrated preparatory cycle
The integrated preparatory cycle (cycle préparatoire intégré, CPI) lasts two years: 4 semesters and 120 ECTS. It is open after a scientific baccalaureate and prepares students for the EMINES engineering cycle. It covers mathematics, physics and chemistry, basic industrial science tools, and reinforced French and English communication.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/formation-ingenieur

## Engineering cycle
The engineering cycle (cycle ingénieur) lasts three years: 6 semesters and 180 ECTS. It is multidisciplinary, with technical, scientific and socio-economic content, team projects and integrated industrial internships. Students enter after scientific preparatory classes (CPGE), a scientific bachelor's degree (licence) or the EMINES integrated preparatory cycle.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/formation-ingenieur https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/offre-de-formation

## Final-year options
In the final year, students choose an option, which EMINES describes as a field of application of industrial management rather than a specialisation. Options currently named on the school's site are Supply Chain Management and Mineral Resources for Energy Transition (both co-supervised with MINES Paris - PSL), and Data Science (taught with École polytechnique). In 2021 the three options were Supply Chain Management, Mining and Data Science. In 2021 the option combined nine weeks of training, conferences and visits with a six-month option study in a host company, ending with a public defence.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/international/partenariats-internationaux https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/images/Documents/brochure_2021.pdf

## Languages
French and English are both used; the preparatory cycle aims for advanced communication in both. English is mandatory for all students, and a second mandatory language is offered (Chinese or Spanish). Language classes use active methods such as corporate theatre, UN simulations, debates and filmed interviews.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/formation-ingenieur https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/formation/formation-ingenieur/l-international-et-les-langues

## Industrial PhD
The industrial PhD (doctorat industriel) starts with a preparatory year (année propédeutique) ending in a "stop or go" evaluation, followed by a three-year doctoral cycle. The preparatory year combines training in industrial management tools, research on a real industrial subject and a pre-internship in the future host company. Applicants need a master's, an engineering degree or equivalent. For 2024 the application deadline was 15 July, with written and oral exams from early September.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/formation/formation-doctorale

## Executive education
EMINES offers executive education for company executives and managers. Training is delivered in-company or tailor-made, using the school's network of experts. The site does not name specific executive programmes.
Source: https://www.emines-ingenieur.org/entreprises/programmes-executives

## Education Fellow preparation
The UM6P Education Fellow preparation (Prépa Education Fellow), run by EMINES since 2019, trains future teachers of mathematics, physics and engineering sciences. It is an intensive one-year programme, renewable once, preparing for the French and Moroccan agrégation exams, with immersion at the Lycée d'Excellence (LYDEX) of Ben Guerir. Participants receive a scholarship of 10,000 dirhams per month.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/prepa-education-fellow

## Specialized master MILEO (older information)
The older English pages of the EMINES site describe MILEO, a specialized master in industrial management and operational excellence co-awarded with MINES ParisTech and accredited by the Conférence des Grandes Écoles in June 2014. It was taught mainly in French, with about 400 hours of courses and a company mission of at least five months. The current French programme list does not include MILEO, so visitors should check with the school whether it is still offered.
Source: https://emines-ingenieur.org/en/education/specialized-masters https://emines-ingenieur.org/formation/offre-de-formation
